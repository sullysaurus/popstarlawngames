Those prices cover the games. Delivery, setup, and pickup are quoted for the venue and timing. Comparing only the number of games can be misleading when the number of simultaneous play stations and service requirements differ.

## What changes a lawn game rental quote?

The largest pricing factors are usually:

- **Game count and size.** Large-format games occupy more vehicle and storage space than tabletop activities.
- **Delivery distance.** A longer round trip adds travel time and operating cost.
- **Site access.** Stairs, elevators, security, parking limits, and long carries can add labor.
- **Rental window.** Late-night or next-day pickup may be quoted separately.
- **Setup complexity.** Several activity zones or a tight installation window require more coordination.
- **Customization.** Signs, scoreboards, branded elements, or custom graphics add production work.
- **Staffing.** Hosted tournaments and field days require planning and on-site personnel beyond equipment delivery.

A detailed event address and timeline will produce a more reliable quote than a guest count alone.

## Delivery service versus pickup rentals

A pickup rental can cost less when the games are compact, the customer has a suitable vehicle, and setup is simple. It can also shift more work onto the host: loading, securing the equipment, storing it overnight, arranging it safely, and returning it on time.

Full-service delivery is useful when the pieces are large, the venue has a narrow setup window, or the event needs a polished presentation. Confirm whether “delivery” includes placement and assembly or merely curbside drop-off. Also ask who moves the games if weather forces the event indoors.

## What do customization and hosting cost?

Simple styling and a coordinated setup are different from fully custom event production. Ask exactly what a package includes before assuming signs, branded games, scoring, or attendants are part of the base price.

For a wedding, customization might mean a central welcome sign or a color detail that connects the setup to the event. For a company gathering, it might include numbered stations, team identifiers, and a scoreboard. The design, materials, approval process, and deadline all affect the quote.

Hosted company tournaments and field days involve another level of service. Popstar custom event production starts at **$2,500** and can include planning, staffing, rotations, scoring, and a run of show tailored to the group and venue.

## Questions to ask before booking

Use the same checklist for every quote:

1. Which exact games are included?
2. Is delivery included, and what geographic area does that cover?
3. Does the team place and assemble every game?
4. How many guest-ready hours are included?
5. When do setup and pickup happen?
6. Are late-night, next-day, stair, or long-carry fees possible?
7. What is the weather-change policy?
8. Is customization included or optional?
9. Does the company carry the insurance your venue requires?
10. Who is responsible for damage, missing pieces, or moving equipment?

The least expensive quote is not always the least expensive event-day option. Consider the vehicle, labor, storage, and coordinator time that a pickup-only price leaves to you.

## Choose based on the event, not just the total

Start with the number of useful stations your space can hold. Then compare service level and timing. A three-game package that arrives styled and ready may create a better guest experience than a larger collection the host has no time to arrange.
