Before booking a lawn game rental company, confirm the exact games, quantities, delivery window, setup responsibility, pickup timing, weather process, surface requirements, and complete price. A clear quote should tell you what is included and what can change.

## Ask what is actually available

Request the game names, quantities, current photographs, and dimensions. If you need duplicate stations for a large crowd, confirm the duplicate quantity rather than assuming a package name provides it.

Ask whether substitutions can occur and how availability is held.

## Clarify delivery and setup

Who unloads, places, levels, and tests the games? What access, parking, elevators, stairs, or security details affect the plan? Get the arrival window and the contact for event-day changes.

Pickup after midnight or the next day may have different pricing and venue requirements.

## Discuss surfaces and weather

Share whether the area is grass, stone, decking, turf, or indoor flooring. Ask which games can move under cover, when the weather decision is made, and whether cancellation or rescheduling terms apply.

## Understand the complete quote

Separate the game price from delivery, setup, pickup, tax, staffing, customization, and special timing. Our [rental cost guide](/blog/lawn-game-rental-cost/) explains the common factors.

## Confirm responsibility and coverage

Ask about supervision, damage, missing pieces, venue insurance requirements, and what guests may move. Equipment rental does not automatically include a host.

## Check the event fit

Provide guest count, ages, timing, space, and goals. A good recommendation should explain why the mix works rather than simply offering the largest package.
