Reserve lawn games as soon as the event date, venue, and general timing are firm. Weddings and peak spring or fall Saturdays benefit from booking several months ahead. A small weekday party may have options closer to the date, but popular packages and delivery windows can fill first.

Availability varies by company and date, so ask rather than relying on a universal deadline.

## Book earlier for constrained events

Plan ahead when the event needs a specific game, several duplicate stations, custom branding, a narrow setup window, late pickup, or a long delivery route. Venue access rules and insurance documents may also require lead time.

Large company events need time to design teams, station rotations, signage, and staffing.

## Gather the useful details first

You do not need a final headcount, but provide the venue address, event date, guest estimate, play window, surface, access, and weather backup. These details produce a more reliable recommendation and quote.

## Ask how the date is held

An inquiry may not reserve equipment. Confirm the deposit, signed agreement, payment schedule, and cancellation terms required to hold the date. Keep the final confirmation with the event plan.

## Reconfirm before event week

Share updated access instructions, planner contacts, weather decisions, and major timeline changes. Do not wait until delivery to mention stairs, a room change, or an earlier ceremony.

## Have a shorter-notice fallback

If the event is close, stay flexible on game mix and pickup timing. Ask what is currently available rather than requesting a highly specific custom package.
