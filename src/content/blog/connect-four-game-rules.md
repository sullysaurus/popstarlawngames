Connect Four is a two-player strategy game. Players alternate dropping one disc into any open column. The first player to connect four of their own discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins. If the grid fills without a four-disc line, the game is a draw.

The same rules work for a giant outdoor version.

## Set up the game

Place the frame on a level surface and confirm the bottom release bar is closed. Divide the discs by color and give one set to each player. A coin toss or quick rock-paper-scissors round can decide who goes first.

Keep extra discs out of walkways. On a windy day, follow the equipment provider’s setup guidance and move the game if the frame cannot remain stable.

## Take a legal turn

On each turn, choose a column and release one disc from the top. The disc falls to the lowest open space in that column. A player cannot move a disc after releasing it or place it directly into a middle space.

Players should alternate without skipping turns. Spectators can offer advice only if both players want team play.

## Recognize a win

Four matching discs in a continuous straight line wins. The line can run left to right, up and down, or on either diagonal. More than four also counts as a win as long as four are connected.

Check the whole grid after each turn. Diagonal wins are the easiest to miss on an oversized frame.

## Reset safely

After the round, support the disc tray and use the frame’s release mechanism as intended. Keep hands and feet clear as the discs fall, collect both colors, and close the bar before the next game.

Children should reset a giant game with an adult. Never climb or hang on the frame.

## Try simple event variations

For team play, two groups can discuss each move and rotate the person who drops the disc. For a fast tournament, play best of three and alternate the starting color. At a cocktail hour, standard single rounds keep the station easy to join.
