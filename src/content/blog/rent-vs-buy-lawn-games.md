Rent lawn games when you need a coordinated event setup, delivery support, several large pieces, or no long-term storage. Buying can make sense when you will use the same small set repeatedly and can transport, maintain, and store it safely.

Compare the full job, not only the equipment price.

## Count future uses honestly

Buying one familiar game for frequent backyard use is different from purchasing five oversized games for one wedding. Estimate realistic uses over the next few years and who will manage them each time.

Storage space and climate matter for wood, fabric, and painted components.

## Include transport

Large boards and frames may require a particular vehicle, lifting help, protective packing, and multiple trips. A low purchase price does not solve event-day transport.

Rental delivery may be quoted separately, so compare both complete totals.

## Consider setup and appearance

Event rentals can provide a coordinated collection and a provider who knows each game’s footprint. Buying gives you control over color and customization but also makes you responsible for assembly, leveling, repairs, missing pieces, and cleanup.

## Think about quantity

A large event may need duplicates for capacity. Purchasing ten stations for one day rarely makes sense unless the organization will reuse them. Renting also allows the mix to change by venue and guest count.
