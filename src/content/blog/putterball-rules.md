Putterball is a head-to-head putting game in which players try to sink balls into the opposing board’s holes. When a player makes a putt, that hole is covered. The first player or team to cover all of the opponent’s holes wins.

Use the instructions supplied with the set for its exact equipment and official variations.

## Set up the boards

Place the two boards opposite one another on a flat surface with room to putt safely. Keep the lane clear of feet, drinks, and passing guests. Carpet, a smooth patio, or even short grass can work differently, so allow a practice putt when the surface changes.

Assign a putter, balls, and board to each side. A coin toss can decide who starts.

## Take turns putting

Players alternate attempts toward the opposite board. A ball must settle in an open hole to count. Cover a made hole with the set’s cap, then return the ball for the next turn.

Do not stand behind a target board where a missed putt could reach you. Waiting players stay outside the lane.

## Win the game

The first side to cover every opposing hole wins. For team play, partners alternate putts. Keep the same order throughout the game so each person receives equal turns.

If an event needs faster rounds, set a time limit and count covered holes when time expires.

## Choose a party format

At open play, use standard head-to-head games and let winners stay for one additional round. For a team station, give each group a fixed number of putts and score total makes. For a wedding cocktail hour, skip the bracket and keep the board available for quick challenges.

## Protect the equipment and guests

Use only the supplied putters and balls. Keep drinks off the boards, do not step on the playing surface, and move the game under cover when rain changes traction. Children should play with an adult nearby.
