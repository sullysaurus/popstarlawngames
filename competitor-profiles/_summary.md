# Lawn Game Inventory Landscape

**Generated:** 2026-08-10

## Market pattern

The local and regional market consistently anchors packages with cornhole, giant tower, and giant four-in-a-row. Operators then add lightweight variety games for package depth and one or two highly visual pieces for premium events.
